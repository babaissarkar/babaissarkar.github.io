About the book

The legend starts
in a smithy

When the Academy's testing caravan arrives at Rik's village, he discovers he has the gift — only to be rejected before he can be properly chosen. Someone interfered. He doesn't yet know who, or why.

Now he must reach the Academy alone to appeal his case. Taking a shortcut through the edge of the southern Sylvan forest, he meets a girl who should not be there. Their encounter lasts only a moment. The bond it leaves behind will last considerably longer.

By the time he understands what has formed between them, it is already too late to undo it. She says she didn't mean for it to happen. She says a great deal of things. Whether any of it is true — and whether the truth, when it finally surfaces, will be better or worse than the lie — is the question at the heart of everything that follows.

A conspiracy already in motion

Rik's rejection wasn't random. Someone made a choice — and didn't expect him to keep walking anyway.

A forbidden bond

Human and Sylvan. User and Conduit. A pairing the world has no framework for — and no patience to tolerate.

Light, returned

Two elements vanished from the world long ago. Their return will not go unnoticed. The question is who notices first — and what they intend to do about it.

The world

Selthrine and beyond

Nations here are not divided by armies alone — but by how they govern the bond between two people, and what they believe it is worth.

Selthrine

Tradition & restraint

A regulated bonded society. The Academy trains Linked pairs here. Stability is the first law.

Sakrenz

Order through control

Bonding is rejected outright. Augmentation fills the gap. A place that would rather suppress a gift than risk what it might become.

Trense

Power through excess

Multiple bonds are permitted. Trense pursues strength without asking what instability costs — and wears it as a virtue.

Rhashin-Kash

Identity through structure

Selective bonding under strict social policy. A dominion remaking itself by controlling who may bond with whom.

The Link system

Two souls.
One current.

A Link is an elemental bond between two people — a User who shapes the energy, and a Conduit who carries it. It requires resonance, emotional and elemental alike.

The Academies exist to find those with the capacity, pair them carefully, and train them before the power exceeds what either can hold alone. Imbalance breaks things. And the old stories about what happens when a bond is forced — or weaponised — are not bedtime tales.

The Sylvans

A people who think
in generations

The Sylvans withdrew from human affairs long ago — not out of weakness, but out of patience. They have watched kingdoms rise and collapse in the time it takes one of their children to reach adulthood.

They do not act quickly. They do not need to. Which is why, when an impossible bond forms at the edge of their forest, they do not look away. Slowly, deliberately, they begin to pay attention.
